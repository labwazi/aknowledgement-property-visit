Proposed acknowledgement terms

For lawyer review before adoption as a binding agreement.

A. Introduction record

The parties acknowledge that Super Deals Mauritius Co Ltd (“Super Deals”) has identified, introduced, arranged access to or facilitated a viewing of the property described in this form. The parties should identify in a separate written agreement the property, parties, scope of protection, applicable duration and any agreed fee or commission.

B. Client acknowledgement

  • I acknowledge Super Deals as the introducing or facilitating agency for this recorded introduction, to the extent accurate and agreed.
  • I agree not to deliberately use a nominee, related entity, intermediary or alternative route for the purpose of evading valid written agency obligations applicable to this introduction.
  • I will disclose any prior direct relationship, previous introduction or other relevant arrangement before proceeding, and will follow the communication and offer process set out in the applicable signed agreement.

C. Owner acknowledgement

  • I acknowledge the recorded introduction and will identify the valid agency appointment and fee terms that apply to this property.
  • I will not knowingly structure a transaction to evade valid, agreed contractual obligations arising from this introduction, and will disclose any prior introduction, competing agency or existing mandate.

D. Participating agency acknowledgement

  • The participating agency will disclose its role and the source of the introduction, respect applicable written agreements and follow agreed procedures for client contact, offers and any co-agency or commission-sharing arrangement.

E. Scope and fairness

These proposed terms are not intended to prevent lawful dealings unrelated to the recorded introduction, override a documented prior relationship, or impose a commission or restriction that has not been validly agreed. Duration, commission, exceptions, remedies and dispute procedures must be set out in a separate lawyer-approved agreement and be enforceable under applicable Mauritius law. No party waives legal rights merely by reading this template.

Typed names and checked boxes in this prototype are not independently verified electronic signatures. Use a suitable e-signature service and approved identity process where required.
